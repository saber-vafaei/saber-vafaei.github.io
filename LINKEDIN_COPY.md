## About
I'm a civil engineer who has spent more than 10 years working across construction sites, technical offices, concrete quality control and geotechnical laboratories.

These days, I supervise civil work at the Cheshmeh Khosh and Dalpari oil fields in Iran, including earthworks, reinforced-concrete foundations, pre-pour checks and site coordination. I enjoy the practical side of engineering: understanding the drawing, checking the details and working with the people responsible for getting it built.

Earlier roles in soil testing, materials quality control and construction documentation helped me see a project from different sides. I work with AutoCAD, ETABS, SAFE and Microsoft Office, and I am currently part of an English-speaking project environment.

I'm interested in connecting with civil construction, infrastructure and oil & gas professionals in Australia, particularly Western Australia. I'm open to relocation and to conversations about site-based roles where my experience may be useful.
