01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE HARD HATGOOD TO MEET YOU

A little about me

I’ve always been more hands-on than hands-off.

Over the years, I’ve worked in site execution, technical offices, geotechnical laboratories and concrete quality control. Different jobs, but they all taught me something about how a project comes together.

Today, I supervise civil activities at oil-well sites in the Cheshmeh Khosh and Dalpari fields. My days involve earthworks, foundations, pre-pour checks, measurements and plenty of conversations with the teams on site.

It’s practical work. And that’s exactly what I enjoy about it.

03 / WHAT I ACTUALLY DOFROM DRAWINGS TO DELIVERY

Different roles, one bigger picture

I care about how things come together.

My experience covers the work on the ground and the technical thinking behind it. These are the areas I’ve spent real time in.

01

Site execution & earthworks

Excavation, grading, compaction, backfilling, reinforced-concrete foundations and daily site coordination.

02

Concrete & quality checks

Reinforcement, formwork, embedded items, anchor bolts, pre-pour inspections and concrete/aggregate QC.

03

Technical office & documentation

Drawings, as-builts, BOQs, quantity take-offs, progress records and contractor payment checks.

04

Soil & materials testing

Geotechnical reporting, soil mechanics tests, compaction control and interpreting construction-material results.

✳

I’ve worked on both sides of the drawing: preparing the documents and standing on the ground where they have to work.
