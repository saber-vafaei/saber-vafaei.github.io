01 — PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

Practical site knowledge.
Strong technical foundations.

I am a Civil Engineer with more than 10 years of experience supporting and supervising construction across oil and gas, industrial, infrastructure and building projects.

In my current role, I supervise civil works at the Cheshmeh Khosh and Dalpari oil fields, including earthworks, excavation, compaction, backfilling and reinforced-concrete foundations. I coordinate with QA/QC, HSE, surveyors and project teams, and check reinforcement, formwork, anchor bolts and embedded items before concrete pours.

My earlier experience in geotechnical testing, concrete quality control and technical offices gives me a broad understanding of construction delivery — from field activities and material testing to drawings, quantities and project documentation.
